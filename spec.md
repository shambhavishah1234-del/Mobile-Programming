# Problem Statement
Bike and car washing and servicing centers often have long queues, especially during busy hours. Customers have to physically visit the center and wait without knowing the exact waiting time. This causes wasted time, overcrowding, and inconvenience for customers. LineLess aims to solve this problem by allowing customers to book appointments, join a virtual queue, and track their vehicle's service status through an app.

# Objectives
To allow customers to book bike and car washing or servicing appointments online.
To provide digital tokens and virtual queue tracking.
To show estimated waiting and service times.
To notify customers when their turn is approaching.
To allow customers to track their vehicle's service status.
To reduce waiting time and overcrowding at service centers.
